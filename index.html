Privacy Policy — Meme Keyboard

Effective date: 1 April 2026
Last updated: 22 November 2026

Data controller / developer: Marios Kanellopoulos
Contact: bmairios@gmail.com
Country: Greece

This policy describes how the mobile application Meme Keyboard (on Android and iOS) (“the App”, “we” in the descriptive sections below) handles information when you use it. The App is a custom input method (keyboard) that lets you use imported images, tags, and optional GIF search while typing in other apps.

1. Information we do not collect (by design)

Based on the current App build:

  • We do not integrate third-party analytics SDKs. The free Android version does show ads through Google AdMob (see section 6).
  • We do not require you to create an account in the App.
  • We do not upload your imported meme images or tags to our servers — the project does not include a backend operated by the developer for that purpose.

2. Data stored on your device

The App stores data locally on your phone or tablet, including:

  • Imported images you choose to add (e.g. from your gallery or the clipboard), in the App’s private storage.
  • Tags and related metadata associated with those imports (stored locally).
  • Settings and preferences (for example: keyboard languages, tag presets, welcome/onboarding state, import limits for the free tier, and keyboard UI preferences).

This data remains on the device unless you remove it (e.g. by deleting imports in the App or uninstalling the App). If you use Back up memes, the App writes your memes and tags to a file at the location you choose; you control that file. Android's system backup may also include App data in your personal Google account backup, if you have it turned on.

3. Clipboard

The App can read image content from the system clipboard when you use features such as “Paste from clipboard” or equivalent flows, and write content to the clipboard when you copy a meme or related content from the keyboard. Clipboard access is used only to provide these features while you use the App.

4. Network and GIPHY (GIF search, Android only)

The App declares Internet access. It is used so the GIF section can request trending and search results from GIPHY (Giphy, Inc.) when a valid GIPHY API key is configured in the build.

When you use GIF search or load trending GIFs:

  • Search terms you type and API requests are sent to GIPHY’s servers over HTTPS.
  • GIPHY’s own privacy policy and terms apply to that service (see Third-party service providers below).

The App shows “Powered by GIPHY” (or similar attribution) in line with typical GIPHY API requirements.

If the API key is missing, GIF features may be unavailable; image import and other keyboard features can still work offline where applicable.

5. In-app purchases (Pro)

The App offers an optional one-time Pro purchase that removes the free import limit and, on Android, ads.

  • Payments are handled entirely by Google Play (Android) or the Apple App Store (iOS). We never see or store your card or payment details.
  • The App only receives confirmation that Pro was purchased, and stores a simple “Pro unlocked” flag on your device. Restoring a purchase asks the store to confirm it again.

6. Advertising (free Android version)

The free Android version shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and process data such as your advertising ID, IP address, device information, and ad interactions in order to deliver, measure, and (where allowed) personalize ads and prevent fraud.

  • If you are in the EEA, UK, or Switzerland, the App asks for your choice through Google's consent message before requesting ads. You can change it at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • The keyboard does not show ads, and what you type is not shared with AdMob.
  • Pro users do not receive ads.

8. Permissions

The App may use or rely on:

  • Internet — to contact GIPHY for GIF content and, in the free Android version, to load ads, as described above.
  • Input method (keyboard) — the App functions as a keyboard; Android system settings control whether the keyboard is enabled and which keyboard is active.
  • Full Access (iOS) — iOS asks you to allow “Full Access” for the keyboard. The App uses it only to read your own image library (shared between the App and its keyboard on your device) and to copy the meme you choose. It does not record what you type and has no network access from the keyboard.
  • Billing — on Android, the Google Play Billing permission is used only for the optional Pro purchase.

No extra sensitive permissions are required for the core behaviors described in the current codebase beyond what the system shows for keyboards and network use.

9. Children’s privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your jurisdiction). If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a child, contact us at the email below and we will assist with deletion where applicable.

10. International users and Greece / EU

The developer is based in Greece. If you use the App in the European Economic Area, you may have rights under the GDPR (e.g. access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection), which you can exercise by contacting us. GIPHY and Google may process data in the United States or other regions according to their respective policies.

If you use the App outside Greece, your information is still primarily processed on your device as described above, except where third parties (GIPHY, Google) are involved as explained in this policy.
